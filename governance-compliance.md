# Governance, Risk & Compliance

Tata kelola, manajemen risiko, standar (ISO 27001, NIST), dan regulasi.

---

- [NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final) — Official NIST security and privacy control catalog for federal information systems and organizations. _(29 Sep 2026)_
- [NIST Cybersecurity Framework](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework) — Official NIST framework for managing and reducing cybersecurity risk through standards, guidelines, and best practices. _(13 Oct 2026)_
- [NIST SP 800-63B Digital Identity Guidelines](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/63/b/upd2/final) — NIST guidance on authentication and digital identity management for secure, compliant identity systems. _(20 Oct 2026)_
- [NIST SP 800-37 Rev. 2](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/37/r2/final) — Official NIST guidance for applying the Risk Management Framework to information systems and organizations. _(26 Oct 2026)_
- [CIS Benchmarks](https://www.cisecurity.org/cis-benchmarks) — Official CIS security configuration benchmarks for hardening systems and improving compliance. _(27 Oct 2026)_
